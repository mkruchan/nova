Connect Monobank

1. Go to api.monobank.ua and log in with your Monobank app to generate a personal token.

This token gives read access to your transactions — it's stored only on this device and is never sent anywhere except Monobank's own servers.

Your synced data stays saved either way — this just controls whether it's shown.
All accounts sync automatically — nothing to select. The first sync grabs your full history; after that it checks for anything new every minute on its own.

Monobank Display Filter
Hides Monobank-synced transactions older than this date. Your manually-entered transactions are never affected, and this has no connection to the main dashboard's own date range.
Remove Data
Deletes every currently-synced Monobank transaction, but keeps your connection active — the next sync pulls your full history back in fresh.
Disconnect
Stops automatic syncing, removes your token from this device, and permanently deletes every transaction synced from Monobank. This can't be undone.
